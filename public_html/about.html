About Hope of Living

Building hope, supporting people and strengthening our community.

Who We Are

Hope of Living is a community-focused initiative based in Lower Philipp, Cape Town. We aim to encourage positive change by connecting people with opportunities, support and resources.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote hope, dignity and community development by making support and opportunities more accessible.

Our Vision

We envision a connected and empowered community where people can learn, grow and work towards a better future.

Our Values

  • Community and togetherness
  • Respect and dignity
  • Hope and encouragement
  • Responsibility and service
